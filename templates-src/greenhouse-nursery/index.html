Care guides

Keep it alive, keep it happy.

Our growers wrote these short guides after twenty-odd years of answering the same five questions at the till. Pick a topic.

  • Nov Move tropicals away from cold windows and radiators.
  • Dec Water less — most houseplants are resting.
  • Jan Wipe dusty leaves so they catch more winter light.

Reading the light in your home

  1. South-facing windows give bright, direct sun — perfect for cacti and olives.
  2. East and west windows suit most tropicals; a sheer curtain softens harsh afternoon sun.
  3. North rooms? Choose snake plants, ZZ plants and ferns.

Grower’s tip: if a plant leans hard toward the window, give it a quarter-turn every time you water.

Watering without worry

  1. Push a finger 3cm into the soil — only water if it feels dry.
  2. Water thoroughly until it drains, then empty the saucer after 15 minutes.
  3. Use room-temperature water; cold water shocks tropical roots.

Grower’s tip: more houseplants die from too much love than too little. When in doubt, wait a day.

The right mix for the right roots

  1. Tropicals love a chunky mix: compost, bark and perlite.
  2. Cacti and succulents need gritty, fast-draining soil.
  3. Feed with a diluted liquid feed every two weeks from spring to early autumn.

Grower’s tip: bring a small bag to our potting bench and we’ll mix custom soil by the scoop.

When (and how) to repot

  1. Roots poking out of drainage holes? It’s time — ideally in spring.
  2. Go up just one pot size (2–5cm wider) to avoid soggy soil.
  3. Water lightly afterwards and keep out of direct sun for a week.

Grower’s tip: we repot anything bought here for free — just bring it back.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

What is the 30-day happy-plant promise?

If a plant from us struggles within 30 days, bring it back with your receipt. We’ll diagnose it and replace it if it can’t be saved.

Do you deliver?

Yes — local delivery within 15 miles from $8, free over $100. Large trees and soil deliveries are booked by phone.

Which plants are safe for cats and dogs?

Look for the paw chip on our labels. Ferns, calatheas, spider plants and African violets are all good choices.

Can I bring my own pots?

Absolutely. Our potting bench will plant into your pot for free when you buy the plant here.

💬 FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Many clients leave a key or door code. Everything is stored securely and you’ll get a message when we arrive and leave.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes — eco-friendly products, vacuums, mops and microfibre cloths are all included. Prefer your own products? Just let us know.

What if something isn’t right?

Tell us within 48 hours and we’ll come back to re-clean that area for free. No forms, no fuss.

Can I cancel or skip a visit?

Yes. Skip, reschedule or cancel anytime with 24 hours’ notice. There are no contracts.

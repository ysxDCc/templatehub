Good to know

Before your first class

Arrive ten minutes early, bring water and wear something you can stretch in. We’ll take care of the rest.

I’ve never done yoga. Where should I start?

Try Lunchtime Reset, Weekend Unwind or the Sunday Basics workshop. Tell your teacher it’s your first time and they’ll offer options throughout.

Do I need my own mat?

No. Clean mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets are provided free in every class.

Is there parking and changing space?

Yes — free street parking after 6pm, two changing rooms with showers and lockers for your valuables.

Can I freeze my membership?

Of course. Pause for up to two months a year, at no cost, straight from your booking email.

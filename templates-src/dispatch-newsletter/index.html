§1 · The paper

What lands on Thursday

One long read

Every week we pick one story and give it the time it deserves — usually 3,000 words, reported on foot, fact-checked twice and never rushed to beat a rival.

The small print

Six short items you won't find elsewhere: council minutes decoded, a price we tracked, a map we drew and the overheard line of the week.

A puzzle & a reply

A hand-set crossword and the letters page, where readers argue with us, correct us and occasionally send poems. We print the best of them.

§5 · Fine print

Questions & answers

When does the newsletter arrive?

Every Thursday at 7:00 a.m. Eastern. Members also get the shorter Sunday edition at 9:00 a.m.

Is the free edition really free?

Yes — the main Thursday letter is free and always will be. Members pay so that it can stay that way.

Do you take advertising?

No ads, no sponsored posts, no tracking pixels. We're funded entirely by readers.

Can I give a gift membership?

Of course. Write to us with the recipient's email and a start date and we'll send a handwritten-style card.

Can I cancel at any time?

One click from any email footer. If you paid yearly, we refund the unused months.

Do you accept pitches?

Yes — we commission four freelance pieces each month and pay $1 a word. Use the form below.

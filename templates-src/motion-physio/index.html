FAQ

Before your first visit

Wear shorts or comfortable clothes, bring any scans or reports, and arrive five minutes early to fill in a short form.

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

No. You can book directly with us. If your insurer requires a referral we’ll tell you before your appointment.

Is my insurance accepted?

We’re registered with most major health insurers and can bill them directly. Bring your policy number to your first session.

How many sessions will I need?

Most people need 3–6. You’ll get an honest estimate at your first appointment, and a written plan by email.

Will treatment hurt?

Some techniques can be a little uncomfortable, but we always work within your limits and explain everything first.

Questions

Before you call.

Is the first conversation free?

A 15-minute call to check we’re the right fit is always free. The full initial consultation is a paid hour, credited against your fee if you instruct us.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. About half our clients meet us by video, and documents can be signed electronically where the law allows.

How quickly will you respond?

A partner replies to every new enquiry within two working days, and existing clients have a direct line to their partner.

What if my matter is outside your practice areas?

We’ll tell you honestly and, where we can, introduce you to a trusted specialist at no cost.

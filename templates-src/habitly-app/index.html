FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Drop us a note below — a real human replies within a day.

Is Habitly really free?

Yes. Starter is free forever with up to five habits and full streak tracking. Plus and Circle add extras, never paywalls on your data.

What happens if I miss a day?

If you have a freeze token, it's used automatically and your streak lives on. If not, the flame resets — but your history and stats stay forever.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Check-ins sync the moment you're back online, and your streak counts the day you actually did it.

Can I export my data?

Any time, as CSV or JSON, from Settings. Your habits are yours.

Is there a student discount?

Students and teachers get Plus for 50% off. Just email us from your school address.

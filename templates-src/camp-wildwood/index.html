For parents

Questions, answered

What if my child gets homesick?

It’s normal, and our counsellors are trained to help. We’ll call you if it lasts, and we never let a camper feel alone.

Can you handle allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. Our kitchen is nut-free and cooks vegetarian, gluten-free and dairy-free meals daily. Tell us on the registration form.

Is financial help available?

About one in five campers receives a campership. Ask for a confidential application — no family is turned away for cost alone.

Can siblings or friends share a cabin?

Friends of similar ages can request each other, and we honour one mutual request per camper wherever possible.

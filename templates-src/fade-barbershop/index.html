Good to know

Questions at the counter

Do I need an appointment?

Nope. Two chairs are always kept for walk-ins. Booking simply guarantees your time and your barber.

How long does a skin fade take?

About 40 minutes including the hot towel finish. The Full Works is closer to an hour.

Do you cut long or curly hair?

Absolutely — Rosa specialises in longer scissor cuts and curls. Mention it when booking so we allow extra time.

Can I pay by card?

Card, cash and phone payments are all welcome. Tips can be added on the card machine.

The Ember method

Slow fire, short road, honest coffee.

Every bag carries the farm, the altitude, the roast date and the name of the person who roasted it. No blends to hide behind, no stale shelf time.

01

Direct from twelve farms

We visit each farm every harvest and pay 40–80% above fair-trade minimums, agreed before the cherries ripen.

02

Roasted every Tuesday

Thirty-kilo batches on our restored 1962 drum roaster, profiled by hand and cupped before a single bag is sealed.

03

Shipped within 48 hours

Compostable valve bags, carbon-neutral delivery and a peak-flavour window printed right on the label.

Good to know

Brewing questions.

Can't find it here? Our roasters answer every email personally.

When will my coffee arrive?

We roast on Tuesdays and ship Wednesday morning. Most orders arrive Thursday or Friday, never more than five days off roast.

Can I pause or skip a delivery?

Yes, any time before Monday midnight from your account page, or just reply to your shipping email and we'll handle it.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh longest, but we'll grind to order for espresso, filter, French press or cold brew at no extra cost.

Is your packaging recyclable?

Our bags are home-compostable, including the valve. Shipping boxes are 100% recycled cardboard with paper tape.

Before & after

Aftercare & FAQAftercare & FAQ

Everything you need to know before your session — and how to keep your new ink looking sharp for decades.

How do I prepare for my session?

Sleep well, eat a proper meal, stay hydrated and skip alcohol for 24 hours. Wear clothes that give easy access to the area.

How should I look after a fresh tattoo?

Keep the film on for 3–5 days, then wash gently twice a day and apply a thin layer of unscented balm. No swimming, sun or picking for two weeks.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes — Kenji and Dove both specialise in them. Send a clear photo of the existing piece with your enquiry.

What’s your minimum age?

You must be 18 or over and bring a valid photo ID to every appointment. No exceptions, even with parental consent.

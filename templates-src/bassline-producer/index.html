FAQ

Fine print, no fluff.

What do I get with a lease?

Untagged WAV and MP3, trackout stems, and rights for up to 500,000 streams and paid performances. Credit "prod. Bassline" and you're good to go.

Can I upgrade to exclusive later?

Yes — your lease fee counts toward the exclusive price as long as nobody else has bought it first.

What format should I send stems in?

24-bit WAV at the session's sample rate, all starting from bar 1, with effects you love printed and a dry copy too.

How fast is mixing turnaround?

Five working days for the first mix, 48 hours for each revision. Rush slots are available for album deadlines.

Do you do in-person sessions?

Yes, by appointment at the studio. Half-day and full-day blocks include an assistant engineer.

The atelier

Patience, tempered to 31°C.

Our chocolatiers paint every mould with coloured cocoa butter, fill it with ganache that rests overnight, and cap it by hand the next morning.

Single-estate cacao

Beans from three family estates in Ecuador, Madagascar and Vanuatu, fermented to our specification and roasted in-house.

Painted by hand

Each shell is airbrushed, splattered or brushed with coloured cocoa butter, so no two bonbons are ever quite alike.

Fresh within 48 hours

Without preservatives our ganaches are at their best for three weeks, so we make every order to ship within two days.

Bean to bonbon

Four days, one bite.

  1. Roast & grind

    Beans are roasted low and slow, then stone-ground for 72 hours.

  2. Paint the shell

    Coloured cocoa butter is brushed and splattered into each mould.

  3. Fill & rest

    Ganache is piped in and left to crystallise overnight at 16°C.

  4. Cap & wrap

    Sealed by hand, boxed in gold and sent to you the same day.

Care & delivery

Before you indulge.

How long do the bonbons keep?

Enjoy them within three weeks of delivery. Store them somewhere cool and dark, between 15 and 18°C, never in the fridge.

Do you ship in warm weather?

Yes. From May to September every box travels in an insulated sleeve with gel packs and ships only Monday to Wednesday.

Can you cater for allergies?

All our bonbons are gluten-free. Our atelier handles nuts and dairy, so we can't guarantee trace-free boxes, but we'll happily build a nut-free selection.

Do you offer corporate gifting?

We create branded sleeves and custom-painted bonbons for orders of 25 boxes or more. Allow three weeks and send us a note below.

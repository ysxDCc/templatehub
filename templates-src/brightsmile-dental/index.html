Insurance & FAQ

Good questions

We bill most major plans directly, so you only pay your share on the day.

PPO plansFamily plansMedicaid kidsHSA / FSA0% finance
I’m really nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Tell us when you book and we’ll schedule extra time, explain every step first and agree a “stop” signal. Gentle sedation is also available.

Do you offer evening and weekend appointments?

Yes — we’re open until 8pm Tuesday to Thursday and 9am–2pm on Saturdays.

What if I don’t have insurance?

Our Smile Club membership is $29 a month and covers two check-ups, two cleans, X-rays and 15% off all treatment.

How soon can I see someone for a toothache?

Call before 2pm and we’ll see you the same day. Out of hours, our emergency line connects you to the on-call dentist.

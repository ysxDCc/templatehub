Taco talk

The line speaks.

Questions?

Good to know.

Can I order ahead?

Yes! Text your order to our number and we'll reply with a pickup time. Pay at the window by card or cash.

What if it rains?

We still serve, just under a bigger awning. Big storms might cancel a stop; we post updates here and by text alert.

Are your tortillas gluten-free?

All our corn tortillas are 100% gluten-free. The Baja fish batter contains wheat, so ask for it grilled instead.

How far ahead should I book catering?

Two to four weeks is ideal. Weekends in May through September fill up fast, so get in touch early.

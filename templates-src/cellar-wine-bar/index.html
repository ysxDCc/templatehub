Before you visit

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask whoever is behind the bar, or write to us below.

Do I need a reservation?

Half our tables are kept for walk-ins. Reservations are recommended on Fridays and Saturdays and for groups of five or more.

Do you serve food?

Small plates every night: cheese, charcuterie, tinned fish, warm bread and a rotating hot dish from our neighbours' kitchen.

Can I host a private event?

The cellar room seats 24 for dinners and tastings. We'll build a custom flight and menu around your budget.

Do you ship bottles?

We deliver mixed cases locally on Wednesdays and ship to most states. Ask about our quarterly cellar club.

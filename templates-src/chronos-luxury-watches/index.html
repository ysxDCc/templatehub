Grand Feu Enameling

Each dial is kiln-fired at 800°C in eight successive layers of vitreous silica enamel, producing a rich, lustrous porcelain finish that never fades across centuries.

Hand Anglage & Beveling

Internal angles polished by hand using gentian wood pegs and diamond paste to achieve mirror reflection according to the Poinçon de Genève standard.

Poinçon de Genève Certified

Every mechanical caliber undergoes 21 days of chronometric trials testing rate variance, magnetic resistance, and water-resistance up to 50 meters.

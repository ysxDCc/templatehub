FAQ

Good to know.

Still unsure? Our move coordinators answer the phone seven days a week.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks is ideal for local moves, four to eight for long distance. We often fit in last-minute moves midweek.

Is my stuff insured?

Basic released-value cover is included. Full-value protection is available on every move for a small additional fee.

Do you move pianos and safes?

Yes — our specialty crew handles pianos, safes, pool tables and large artwork with the right equipment.

What can’t you move?

Hazardous materials, perishable food and plants on long-distance moves. We’ll send a full list after booking.

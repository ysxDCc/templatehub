Chapter one

Three reasons to stay a while

We’re a neighbourhood bookshop first, a café second and an excuse to sit down for a long time third.

01 / Books

Hand-picked shelves

Every title is chosen by a human who read it. Special orders arrive in two to four days, wrapped in brown paper.

02 / Café

The Reading Room café

Small-batch coffee, loose-leaf tea and slabs of cake. Bring any book to the table — even one you haven’t paid for yet.

03 / Events

Evenings worth leaving the house for

Author readings, a silent book club, poetry-and-pie nights and a Saturday story hour for small listeners.

The Reading Room

A café that expects you to linger

Free Wi-Fi, plenty of plugs, and a quiet corner marked with a small brass sign that says “Shh — chapter ending”.

Fine print

Questions, answered

Can you order a book you don’t stock?

Almost certainly. Ask at the till or use the form below — most special orders arrive in two to four working days and we’ll text you when it’s in.

Do you buy second-hand books?

On the first Monday of each month we look through pre-loved books for store credit. Good-condition fiction and children’s books are most welcome.

Is the shop accessible?

The ground floor, café and accessible toilet are step-free. Events upstairs have a lift; let us know if you need anything else.

Do you do gift cards and book subscriptions?

Yes — gift cards from $10, and a “Surprise Me” subscription from $22 a month where a bookseller picks a title just for you.

Come find us

Order a book, book a table, or just say hello

Fill in the library card and a bookseller will reply within a day — usually with a recommendation you didn’t ask for.

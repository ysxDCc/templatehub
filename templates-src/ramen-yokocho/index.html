Before you queue

Good to know

Do you take reservations?

We're walk-in only for the counter. Groups of 6 to 12 can book the whole bar from 6 to 8pm by emailing us.

How long is the wait?

Usually 10 to 20 minutes after 10pm on weekends. Leave your number at the door and we'll text you when a seat opens.

Vegan or gluten-free options?

Midnight Garden is fully vegan. Gluten-free rice noodles can be swapped into any bowl for $1.

Can I order takeaway?

Yes. Broth and noodles are packed separately so nothing goes soggy on the way home.

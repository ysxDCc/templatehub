How far ahead should we book?

Most couples book 10–16 months ahead. Elopements and weekday weddings often have shorter notice availability — just ask.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes, happily. Travel within 100 miles is included; beyond that I quote flights and lodging at cost, with no markup.

When will we see our photos?

A sneak peek arrives within 72 hours and your full gallery within five weeks, ready to download and share.

Can we add hours on the day?

Of course — extra coverage is $350 per hour and can be arranged in the moment if the party is too good to leave.

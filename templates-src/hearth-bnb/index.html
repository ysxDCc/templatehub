Why guests return

Small comforts, done properly

We only have three rooms, so there’s always time for a proper chat, a second pot of tea and directions to the best view in the valley.

Farmhouse breakfasts

Eggs from our hens, bread baked at dawn and honey from the orchard hives.

Log fires & armchairs

A crackling hearth in the snug every evening, with board games and a well-stocked bookcase.

Trails from the door

Five waymarked walks from 2 to 12 miles, with hand-drawn maps and packed lunches on request.

House notes

  • Two-night minimum at weekends

Are dogs welcome?

Yes, in the Orchard Room — up to two well-behaved dogs, with a bed, bowls and a towel for muddy paws. $15 per stay.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Of course. Tell us when you book and we’ll plan vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or dairy-free breakfasts.

Is there Wi-Fi and phone signal?

Wi-Fi in the farmhouse, patchy signal in the hut — most guests call it a feature, not a bug.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. After that we’ll happily move your dates instead.

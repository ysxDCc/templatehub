FAQ

Need to know.

Will talks be recorded?

Yes. Every main-stage talk is captioned live and recordings go to all ticket holders within a week.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and we enforce it. Trained staff are on site and reachable by phone throughout both days.

Can I transfer my ticket?

Free name changes until May 1, 2027. Refunds are available until March 31.

Is the venue accessible?

Step-free throughout, with reserved seating, quiet rooms, live captions and ASL interpreters on the main stage.

Harborline Hall

Pier 41 Promenade, San Francisco, CA. Six minutes' walk from the train, with a partner hotel block across the street.

Delivery & care

Questions, gently answered.

Still unsure? Call the shop and a florist will pick up.

What areas do you deliver to?

Same-day delivery covers the whole city within 8 miles of the shop. Next-day delivery reaches the wider county for $14.

Can I choose a delivery time?

We deliver between 11am and 7pm. For a specific one-hour window, add timed delivery at checkout for $10.

How do I keep my flowers fresh?

Trim stems at an angle every two days, change the water and keep the vase away from fruit and radiators. Every bouquet comes with flower food.

What if the flowers don't last?

Our seven-day freshness promise means we'll replace any bouquet that wilts early. Just send us a photo.

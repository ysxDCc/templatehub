Questions, answered

Do you see emergencies?

We see urgent cases during opening hours with same-day slots. Out of hours, our phone line redirects to the nearest 24-hour animal hospital.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — our Pawprint Care Club spreads routine care over monthly payments from $19, and we can split larger bills.

Can I stay with my pet during the exam?

Always, for consults. For surgery and dental work, you can say goodbye at the door and we’ll send updates.

Which animals do you treat?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets and small reptiles. Ask us about anything else!

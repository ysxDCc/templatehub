What we do

Three programmes, one community

Everything is free to the people who use it, and everything is shaped by the neighbours we serve.

Food bank

The Harbour Pantry

A choice-based pantry open five days a week, with fresh produce, halal and allergy-friendly shelves, and home delivery for elders.

Youth

Lighthouse Youth Club

Homework help, mentoring, cooking classes and weekend sailing for 11–18 year olds — with a hot dinner every session.

Kitchen

Community Kitchen

Pay-what-you-can lunches every weekday, cooked by volunteers and trainee chefs from our employment course.

Questions

Good to know

How do I get food from the pantry?

Just come in during opening hours with any proof of address. No referral is needed for your first three visits.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Hope Harbor is a registered charity and you’ll receive an emailed receipt for every gift.

Can I donate food instead of money?

Absolutely — drop off non-perishables Mon–Sat, 9am–5pm. Our current most-needed list is posted at the door.

Can my company volunteer as a team?

We host team days for groups of 6–20 on weekdays. Get in touch to book a date.

Get in touch

Need help, want to help, or just have a question?

Send us a message and the right person on our team will get back to you within two working days.

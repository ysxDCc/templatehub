Good to know

Festival info

Everything else lives in the festival guide we email two weeks before gates open.

Is there an age limit?

All ages are welcome. Under-16s must be with a ticketed adult, and kids under 8 go free.

How do I get there?

Free shuttles run every 20 minutes from Bayfield station. Car parking must be booked in advance; bikes park free.

Will I actually see an aurora?

We can't promise the sky, but late August has good odds — and our light installations make sure every night glows anyway.

Is the site accessible?

Yes: accessible viewing platforms, campsite, toilets and a free companion ticket. Contact us to arrange support.

What's the refund policy?

Full refunds until June 1, 2027, and free ticket transfers until August 1 through your account.

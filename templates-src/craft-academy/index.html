Good questions

Before you pick up a saw

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Module 1 assumes you've never owned a hand tool and builds up from there.

How much space do I need?

A sturdy table or a fold-down bench in a 2 × 2 metre corner is enough for every project in the course.

What will tools cost me?

Around $180 for the essential set if you buy second-hand using our guide — or choose the Workshop Kit and we send them.

How long do I have access?

Forever, including all future updates and new bonus projects.

What if it isn't for me?

Email us within 30 days for a full refund. Keep the plans.

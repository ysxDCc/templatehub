Questions

Good to know before you come

Where do I park?

Free parking is in our lot on Elm Street, with accessible spaces and a drop-off zone right by the main entrance.

Is the building accessible?

Yes. Step-free access throughout, an accessible restroom, hearing loop in the main hall and large-print song sheets on request.

Do I have to give money?

Not at all. Guests are never expected to give. Members support our work and community programs through regular giving.

Can I rent the hall for an event?

Our community centre rooms are available for local groups, classes and celebrations at low cost. Ask us for availability.

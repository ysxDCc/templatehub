Before you go

Good questions

Still wondering? Call the ski school desk — we answer from 7:30am every day of the season.

What should I wear?

Waterproof jacket and trousers, thermal base layers, gloves (not mittens for boarders), goggles and sun cream. Helmets come with every lesson.

What if the weather is bad?

We teach in light snow and wind. If lifts close for safety we reschedule your lesson or refund it in full — your choice.

Can my child join mid-week?

Yes. Snow Cubs and Shred Crew run every day, so kids join the group that matches their level on any morning.

Do you teach adaptive skiing?

We have four adaptive instructors and sit-ski equipment. Get in touch so we can plan the right session together.

Biochemical Architecture

How Point-of-Care CRISPR Cleavage Operates

STEP 01 // SAMPLE LYSIS & RPA

Isothermal Pre-Amplification

Crude patient specimen (nasal swab, whole blood, or saliva) is injected directly into the cartridge inlet. Lyophilized Recombinase Polymerase Amplification (RPA) enzymes amplify target nucleic acids at a constant 37°C without thermal cyclers.

STEP 02 // GUIDE RNA BINDING

Ultra-Specific gRNA Interrogation

Engineered synthetic crRNA identifies single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) and specific genetic pathogen footprints with zero off-target cross-reaction, distinguishing closely related viral strains within minutes.

STEP 03 // COLLATERAL REPORTERS

Fluorophore-Quencher Cleavage

Target recognition triggers non-specific ssDNA/ssRNA trans-cleavage activity in Cas12a/Cas13a, disintegrating thousands of fluorescent quencher probes per second for an exponentially magnified optical readout.

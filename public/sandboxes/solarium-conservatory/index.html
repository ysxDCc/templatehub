CRITICALLY ENDANGERED ACC-2018-ARUM-01

Titan Arum

Amorphophallus titanum

Native to the relictual rainforests of western Sumatra. Producing the largest unbranched inflorescence on earth, blooming unpredictably once every 7 to 10 years for a single 48-hour nocturnal reproductive cycle.

SUBSTRATE Volcanic Loam / Pumice
LAST BLOOM CYCLE October 2024
CORM WEIGHT 82.4 kg
POLLEN VIABILITY 97.2% Cryo-Banked
CRITICALLY ENDANGERED ACC-2021-ORCH-88

Ghost Orchid

Dendrophylax lindenii

Leafless epiphytic orchid indigenous to wet cypress swamps of Cuba and southwestern Florida. Photosynthesis is carried out exclusively through an intricate network of photosynthetic cord-like roots host-bonded with mycorrhizal fungi.

HOST PHOROPHYTE Fraxinus caroliniana
SYMBIONT Ceratobasidiaceae #7
CANOPY HEIGHT 4.2 m
SPECIMEN SPEC Wild Reintroduced
CRITICALLY ENDANGERED ACC-1996-WOLM-03

Wollemi Pine

Wollemia nobilis

The "living fossil" conifer rediscovered in 1994 inside a remote gorge of the Blue Mountains, Australia. Fewer than 80 mature specimens survive in habitat. Features remarkable bubbly bark reminiscent of boiling chocolate.

CLADE Araucariaceae
PHYLOGENETIC AGE 90+ Million Years
HABITAT ALTITUDE 650 m
CANOPY SPREAD 8.4 m Multi-Trunk
EXTINCT IN THE WILD ACC-1803-FRNK-12

Franklin Tree

Franklinia alatamaha

Extinct in the wild along the Altamaha River in Georgia since the early 19th century. Every specimen living in global botanical sanctums descends from seed propagated by royal botanists John and William Bartram in 1777.

FAMILY Theaceae (Tea family)
FLOWERING WINDOW Late Aug – Frost
WILD POPULATION 0 Specimens
GENETIC REPOSITORY Pure F1 Lineage

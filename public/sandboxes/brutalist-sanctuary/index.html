The Physics of Heavy Mass

01

Diurnal Heat Flywheel

650mm cast-in-place walls absorb direct high-altitude solar radiation throughout peak afternoon hours, holding up to 1.8 megajoules per cubic meter and radiating gentle radiant warmth into living volumes over twelve nighttime hours.

02

Subterranean Bedrock Coupling

Pavilions are socketed directly into native granite or basalt bedrock through 32mm titanium anchor studs. Deep geo-exchange maintains an unshifting ground temperature baseline of 11.2°C year-round without mechanical chillers.

03

Acoustic & Electromagnetic Null

Dense aggregate and high slump concrete create complete 52 dB acoustic dampening from sub-zero winds and freezing sleet. The absence of drywall, studs, or synthetic cavity insulation guarantees absolute stillness.

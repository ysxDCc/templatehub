FAQ

Common Questions

Do you accept international patients? +
Yes. We have a dedicated International Patient Services team that assists with travel, accommodation, medical visa letters, and language interpretation in 24 languages.
What insurance plans do you accept? +
We are in-network with major insurance providers including Bupa, AXA, Allianz, and most national health insurance plans. Contact us to verify your specific coverage.
How quickly can I get an appointment? +
Routine consultations are typically available within 48 hours. Emergency same-day appointments are available — call our priority line or use the booking form.
Are telemedicine consultations available? +
Yes. Video consultations are available for follow-up appointments, second opinions, and non-emergency conditions through our secure patient portal.

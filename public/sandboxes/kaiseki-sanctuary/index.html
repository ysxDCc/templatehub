COURSE 01 // SAKIZUKE (先付) 早春の先付
Steamed Abalone with Liver Sauce & Wild Mountain Bracken

Awabi gently simmered for six hours in pure sake and kombu dashi, served over hand-harvested wild kogomi greens with an emulsion of abalone liver and cold-pressed sesame oil.

📍 Line-caught off Shimane Peninsula; Foraged Kogomi from Mount Hiei
COURSE 02 // HASSUN (八寸) 早緑の八寸
Seasonal Seasonal Morsels of Land and Sea

Simmered octopus with bamboo shoot, Tai sea bream roe encased in mountain yam cake, grilled river sweetfish marrow, and butter-braised Kyoto heirloom Kintoki carrots.

📍 Kamigamo Heirloom Farm, Northern Kyoto Basin
COURSE 03 // WANMONO (椀物) 清汁仕立て
Clear Dashi Broth with Amadai Tilefish & Shogoin Turnip

The spiritual heart of kaiseki. Dashi extracted at 82°C from 3-year Rishiri kelp and freshly shaved Makurazaki katsuobushi flakes, poured over charcoal-seared Wakasa bay tilefish.

📍 50m Yasaka Deep Spring Water; Rishiri Aged Kombu (Rebun Island)
COURSE 04 // MUKOZUKE (向付) 本日の造里
Ikejime Akashi Sea Bream & Bluefin Otoro

Served with freshly grated wild wasabi from Gotemba and 2-year naturally fermented dark cedar barrel soy sauce. Accompanied by tender baby sea kelp.

📍 Akashi Strait Tidal Currents; Shizuoka Mountain Wasabi
COURSE 05 // YAKIMONO (焼物) 備長炭焼
Bincho Charcoal-Grilled A5 Miyazaki Beef & Kamo Eggplant

Seared over Kishu white binchotan coals to caramelize natural marrow, accompanied by roasted round Kamo eggplant basted in red Saikyo sweet miso paste.

📍 Miyazaki Prefecture A5 BMS 11; Kyoto Kamigamo Kamo Nasu
COURSE 06 // CHIIBATHI (中鉢) 酢の物 口替り
Snow Crab & Mozuku Seaweed in Tosazu Gelee

📍 Echizen Harbor, Fukui Prefecture
COURSE 07 // TAKIAWASE (炊合) 春筍の炊合

Dug before dawn from the clay hills of Tsukahara, braised simply with rice bran and dried bonito flakes, topped with aromatic crushed sansho pepper leaf.

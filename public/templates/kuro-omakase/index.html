Thirty-Five Year Craft
「魚と米と水。それ以上でもそれ以下でもない。」
"Fish, rice, and water. Neither more, nor less. In restraint lives infinity."

Master Kenjiro Takahashi apprenticed for 18 years in Tsukiji before founding KURO. His single-bevel yanagiba knives are forged by hand in Sakai using white paper steel (Shirogami #1) quenched in water at midnight.

Atelier Architectural Elements

  • • Shou Sugi Ban: Traditional flame-charred Japanese cedar walls absorbing ambient acoustic vibration.
  • • Wajima-nuri Lacquerware: Hand-turned urushi lacquer vessels seasoned over twelve months.
  • • Niigata Koshihikari Rice: Single-harvest grain cooked in clay donabe pots with mountaintop soft water.
  • • Bizen Ware Ceramics: Wood-fired stoneware unglazed for natural earthy texture.
